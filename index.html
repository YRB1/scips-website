Who we are

Built by residents, for residents.

SCIPS is a well-established charity that's led and governed by the community. We help people come together, build pride in their neighbourhoods and get a real say in how their homes and services are run.

  • Tenants & leaseholders

    Understand your rights, raise issues and take part in decisions about your housing.

  • Tenants' & residents' associations

    From starting a TRA from scratch to keeping an established one running well.

  • Community groups

    Guidance, training and a route to the people who make decisions.

What we do

Practical help that gets residents heard.

01

Group support

Hands-on help to set up and run a residents' or community group properly.

  • Writing a constitution and electing a committee
  • Running meetings and AGMs
  • Finding funding and managing money
02

Consultation & participation

We co-ordinate consultation so residents' views reach the council and housing providers, and come back as real change.

03

Information

Clear, plain-English information on housing, your rights and what's happening locally.

04

Training

Free sessions for committee members and volunteers, from chairing meetings to understanding housing.

05

Advocacy

When something isn't right, we'll help you put your case to the people who can fix it.

Start a group

Thinking about starting a residents' association?

You don't need experience. You need a few neighbours who care, and we'll help with the rest.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Call, email or use the form below. Tell us where you live and what's going on in your area.

  2. 2

    Gather your neighbours

    We'll help you reach other residents and plan a first meeting in a local venue.

  3. 3

    Set up properly

    We'll guide you through a constitution, electing a committee and opening a bank account.

  4. 4

    Get recognised and get heard

    Once you're up and running, we'll help you build a relationship with your landlord and the council, and keep supporting you as you grow.

    Ready to start?

    It all begins with one conversation.

FAQs

Common questions.

Can't see your question? Call us on 0121 544 1230.

Does it cost anything to get help from SCIPS?+

No. Our support is free for tenants, residents and community groups in Sandwell.

Are you part of Sandwell Council?+

No. SCIPS is an independent charity, led and governed by the community. We work closely with the council and housing providers so residents' views are heard.

Do I need to be a council tenant?+

No. We support tenants, leaseholders and residents across the borough, plus the community groups that serve them.

How do I get referred?+

You don't need a referral. Just call 0121 544 1230, email enquiries@scips.org.uk, or send us a message using the form below.

Can you come to us?+

Yes. As well as our office in West Bromwich, we work from community venues across Sandwell when needed.
