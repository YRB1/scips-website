Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for SCIPS to review. This page was written for the website concept and must be checked by SCIPS (and, if needed, a legal adviser) before the site goes live.

Who we are

Sandwell Community Information and Participation Service Limited ("SCIPS", "we") is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. Contact: enquiries@scips.org.uk, 0121 544 1230, or 2nd Floor, National Metalforming Centre, 37–47 Birmingham Road, West Bromwich B70 6PY.

What we collect and why

WhatWhyLawful basis
Name, email address and your message (required)To reply to your enquiryConsent, which you give with the tick box on the form
Phone number and town (optional)To call you back if you'd prefer, and to send your enquiry to the right personConsent

We only ask for what we need. We don't use your details for marketing, we don't sell them, and we don't share them with anyone else unless the law requires it or you ask us to (for example, to raise an issue with your landlord on your behalf).

How the contact form works

When you press "Send message", the form opens your own email app with your enquiry filled in. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to help you, and then delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to:

  • see the information we hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • have your information deleted
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • withdraw your consent at any time

To make a request, including a request to delete your data, email enquiries@scips.org.uk with the subject "Data request" or call 0121 544 1230. We'll respond within one month.

Children

If you're under 13, please ask a parent, carer or trusted adult to contact us for you.

Other websites and services

Fonts and images on this site are stored on our own server. The map is provided by OpenStreetMap and only loads if you choose to show it. The "Translate this page" option sends you to Google Translate in a new tab. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
