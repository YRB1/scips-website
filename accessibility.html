Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for SCIPS to review. This page was written for the website concept and must be checked by SCIPS (and, if needed, a legal adviser) before the site goes live.

SCIPS wants as many people as possible to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What you can do on this site

  • make the text bigger (A, A+ and A++ buttons)
  • switch on high contrast, a more readable font, or underlined links
  • stop slideshows and animations (the site also follows your device's "reduce motion" setting)
  • use the whole site with a keyboard, including the menu, carousels and forms
  • use a screen reader, with descriptive alt text on meaningful images
  • translate the page using Google Translate

What we've checked

  • Colour contrast of text and buttons against WCAG AA
  • Keyboard focus is always visible
  • Pop-ups can be closed with the Escape key and keep keyboard focus inside them

Known issues

Translations come from Google Translate and may not be perfect.

Need something in a different format?

If you need information in another format, such as large print, easy read or another language, or you find a problem with this site, call 0121 544 1230 or email enquiries@scips.org.uk.

Enforcement

If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).
