Handling money openly and carefully protects your committee and keeps members' trust. You don't need to be an accountant. You need a few simple habits.

## Set up the basics

- **Open a bank account in the group's name**, never a personal account
- **Have at least two people approve every payment**, and make sure they aren't related or living together
- **Agree a spending limit** for the treasurer between committee meetings

## Keep simple records

- Record every payment in and out, with the date, amount and what it was for
- Keep receipts and invoices for everything you spend
- Pay money into the bank promptly rather than holding cash

A simple spreadsheet or cash book is enough for most groups.

## Report to members

- The treasurer should give a short update at each committee meeting
- Present the year's accounts at your annual general meeting
- If your constitution asks for it, have the accounts checked by someone independent of the committee

## Applying for grants

Funders will usually ask for your constitution, your bank details and your latest accounts. Keep copies ready. Only spend grant money on what it was given for, and keep evidence of how you spent it.
