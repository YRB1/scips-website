Your annual general meeting (AGM) is when members hear what the group has done over the year, see the accounts and elect the committee. Always check your constitution first: it sets out the rules your AGM must follow.

## Before the AGM

- **Check your constitution** for how much notice you must give, the quorum and how elections work
- **Book a venue** that's accessible and easy for residents to reach
- **Send out notice** to all members in good time, with the date, time, place and agenda
- **Prepare the reports:** a chair's report on the year and a treasurer's report on the money
- **Get the accounts checked** by someone independent of the committee, if your constitution asks for it
- **Invite nominations** for the committee and officer roles

## A typical agenda

1. Welcome and apologies
2. Minutes of last year's AGM
3. Chair's report
4. Treasurer's report and accounts
5. Election of officers and committee
6. Any changes to the constitution (if notice was given)
7. Any other business

## On the day

- Have a signing-in sheet so you can check the meeting is quorate
- Ask someone who isn't standing for election to oversee the elections
- Make sure someone takes minutes, including the results of every vote

## After the AGM

- Write up and share the minutes
- Update your bank's records if the people approving payments have changed
- Let your landlord and the council know who is on your new committee
