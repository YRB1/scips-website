Cookie policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for SCIPS to review. This page was written for the website concept and must be checked by SCIPS (and, if needed, a legal adviser) before the site goes live.

The short version

This website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

What we store on your device

NameTypePurpose
scips-consentLocal storageRemembers your choice about external content so we don't ask again
scips-scale, scips-hc, scips-readable, scips-calm, scips-linksLocal storageRemembers the text size and accessibility settings you chose
scips-seenLocal storageShows the opening animation only on your first visit

These are only used to make the site work the way you asked, never to track you, and they stay on your device. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

External content

The map on our contact section comes from OpenStreetMap. It only loads if you press "Allow external content" or "Load map". When it loads, OpenStreetMap will receive your IP address.

Changing your choice

Use the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of any page to change your choice.
